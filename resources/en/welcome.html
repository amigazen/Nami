Ukiyo · floating world

Wave, not AmiSomething

Nami (波) means wave — a NetSurf fork for AmigaOS, printed in Great Wave blue against coral and washi.

  • Native chrome

    Optional Nami UI style with in-window close, zoom and depth gadgets, AWeb-style border scrollers, and a boingball throbber.

  • Light engine

    HTML and CSS from Hubbub and LibCSS — real layout without dragging a full modern stack onto classic or OS4 hardware.

  • Amiga roots

    ARexx, themes, local MIME types, Workbench drag-save, and the Choices you expect from a proper Amiga browser.
